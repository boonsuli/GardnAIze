# Claude — independent critical review
Apply the same GardnAIze common protocol and source registry. Review Gemini's supplied profiles for the same taxa, not a different subset. Before accepting a claim, assess biological plausibility and seek independent documentary evidence where browsing is available. Do not trust Gemini's URLs or claim to have accessed pages you cannot retrieve. For each trait report scientific_name, trait, verdict (SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, UNCERTAIN), justification, exact supporting URLs, and proposed_value (null if insufficient evidence). Explicitly distinguish plausibility from verified evidence. Check cultivar identity, units, climate, dimensions and conditional cultivation. Do not modify original Gemini data. All findings remain pending human review; no expert labels or automatic approval.
